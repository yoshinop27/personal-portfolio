Recent Projects

Baseball Trainer

An AI-powered baseball swing analysis platform that uses computer vision to analyze player swings and provide detailed biomechanical feedback. The system processes video frames through OpenRouter for advanced analysis, then leverages Google Gemini to generate personalized coaching recommendations. With ElevenLabs integration, players receive feedback through a personalized voiceover coach. The app also integrates Blast Motion sensors to capture real-time swing data including bat speed, exit velocity, and launch angle, rendering live results directly in the application.

TypeScript Python Next.js React Flask MediaPipe YOLOv8 MongoDB Auth0
